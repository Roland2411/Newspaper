LOCAL

Major River Restoration Project Gets Green Light

By Jessica Morales — Today the city council approved a $45 million plan to restore the Eastbank river corridor, funding habitat reconstruction, a continuous riverside trail, and new floodplain parks. Planners say the work will take five years and include community-led stewardship programs to ensure long-term success.

Local Newsroom Adopts AI Tools to Speed Reporting

By Aaron Kim — The Gazette has launched an initiative to integrate AI-assisted transcription and translation tools into daily operations, freeing reporters to focus on verification and deeper reporting. Editors emphasize that fact-checking and human judgment remain central to editorial standards.
